How are these scores calculated?
GP Score
(27 − Great Power Rank + 1) × 75, floored at 0 for rank 28+. Rank 1 (strongest) scores highest; only the top 27 ranked countries score anything.
War Score
Per finished war: reward for winning against more Enemies with fewer Allies to share credit with; penalty for losing scaled the same way. A war with no real combatant on either side (a hired condottieri contract) uses a flat ±2 instead.
Llama Points
Llama Points = (GP Score ÷ 100) + ∑ War Score across every one of the player's scored wars this campaign.
White Peace
When nothing in the data points to a winner either way, the war is scored as a flat 0 for both sides rather than guessing - shown separately below from wars that were actually decided.

Player Wars

One row per war between players - who fought whom, who won, and how much score each side gained or lost.
