Llama Score User Manual
  1. Download the Llama Score Dashboard (the button at the top of this page) and choose the installer for your computer: Setup.exe on Windows, the matching Intel/Apple Silicon .dmg on macOS, or the matching .deb/.rpm on Linux.
  2. Install and open Llama Score Dashboard, then leave it running while you play. Its Settings button controls the watched save, game-install, and campaign-data folders:
    • EU5 save folder - where it watches for new autosaves. Defaults to your own Documents\Paradox Interactive\Europa Universalis V\save games. Only change this if your saves live somewhere non-default (a different drive, a custom Documents location, etc).
    • Ledger data folder - where it records what it sees. Defaults to Documents/Llamabeg/Campaign Data, independent of the application installation, so updates and uninstallation do not remove campaign history.
  3. Play normally. EU5 autosaves periodically; the Dashboard reads each new one as it lands and builds up a running log of every war in your campaign - including wars that get cleared out of the save itself before you'd otherwise catch them as "concluded."
  4. Come back to this tab any time you want to check scores. Click "Connect campaign folder…" below (one-time per browser) and pick Documents/Llamabeg/Campaign Data - after that, loading any save from a linked campaign shows its Llama Score automatically, no more picking required.
  5. Multiplayer note: there's no server and no automatic syncing between different players' computers - each person who wants to see live scores runs their own copy of the Dashboard against their own save folder. Since everyone's client is autosaving the same shared campaign, everyone ends up tracking the same wars independently.

How are these scores calculated?
GP Score
(27 − Great Power Rank + 1) × 75, floored at 0 for rank 28+. Rank 1 (strongest) scores highest; only the top 27 ranked countries score anything.
War Score
10·E·W/(A+1) + 10·(W−1)·(A+1)/(2·E) (E = Enemies, A = Allies, W = 1 if won else 0) - or a flat ±2 when E=0 (a condottieri contract with no real combatant). PVE: a vassal fighting alongside its own overlord isn't a separate Enemy/Ally (beating "the Ottomans + 3 vassals" = beating "the Ottomans"); a vassal fighting entirely on its own still counts normally.
How a winner is decided
EU5 clears the in-game war score once a war concludes, so nothing in the save directly says who won. Only three signals ever decide a winner, in priority order: an enforced war reparations payment (peace-treaty term - the payer lost), a war goal of literal independence (is the vassal still subject afterward?), or a real land transfer between the war's two original sides (territory that measurably changed hands, not just occupied mid-war). Everything else - war score, battle losses, occupation, treasury/prestige swing - shows as "considered but not decisive," never used to pick a side: each produced real false positives on tested campaign data.
Llama Points / Alpaca Points
(GP Score ÷ 100) + ∑ War Score across every one of the player's scored wars this campaign - called Llama Points in PVP mode and Alpaca Points in PVE mode so the two leaderboards are never confused for one another, even though it's the exact same formula either way.
White Peace
None of the three decisive signals fire → flat 0 for both sides, shown separately from decided wars. Expected for a war fought mostly through allies/vassals, not a sign the algorithm gave up - the "How decided" tooltip still shows what it considered, and "Edit war corrections" overrides any case where the real outcome was clearer in-game.
Which wars come out clearest
Clean conquest (territory shrinks/grows) is most reliable. Indemnity peace and independence wars are equally reliable since both read a direct peace-treaty fact, needing no territory to change hands at all. A war resolved only through allies, or genuinely inconclusive, falls to White Peace by design - even if war score or battle losses "look" one-sided.
Single save vs. a connected campaign ledger
A single save with no Dashboard/recorder data has no "before the war" snapshot to diff, so scoring falls back to a rough guess (whoever occupies more contested land right now) - treat those numbers as estimates. Connecting the Llama Score Dashboard's campaign folder unlocks the real detection above: it snapshots each war right before/after it disappears from the save (exactly the before/after comparison reparations, independence, and land transfer need) and catches wars EU5 purges before your next autosave otherwise would.
Why is a war greyed out / marked "auto"?
A war row is auto-excluded from scoring (an auto badge - hover it for the exact reason) when:
  • Not a PvP result - the opposing side was never player-controlled.
  • Not a PvE result (PVE mode only) - the opposing side had a real player.
  • Player had already left - this player's country had already reverted to AI before the war even began. A war they were actually playing when it started still counts, even if they left partway through it.
  • Opponent had already left - every enemy in the war had already left the campaign before it even began. A war fought against a real opponent still counts even if that opponent left partway through it.
  • Never actually fought (PvP only) - joined the war but never recorded a Battle/Capture loss.
  • Manually hidden - this player was hidden as departed on the Players table.
Every one of these can be overridden per-war in "Edit war corrections" below if the automatic read is wrong for a specific war.
